## Final prompt

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Asset: replacement artwork for the Create Your Own Map game-mode card.
Image roles: Image 1 is the map-creation concept to rework. Images 2 and 3 are the existing game's artwork and are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not targets to alter.
Rework image 1 to fit seamlessly into the cinematic game-art family of images 2 and 3. The first concept is too much like a busy astronomy chart. Match the existing cards' atmospheric painterly realism, dramatic physical scale, restrained starfield, smoky deep-space background, strong readable silhouettes, cyan edge light and warm distant sun, and broad shadowed lower third.
New composition: a single prominent blue-grey rocky planet being created or shaped, positioned in the upper center-right, with realistic dark terrain and clouds. One edge of its globe is still an elegant translucent cyan construction wireframe, with just two or three thin cyan orbital planning arcs and a few small luminous planetary nodes nearby. A tiny angular survey vessel with a cyan engine in the middle distance adds physical scale and ties the image into the existing fleet artwork; it must stay subordinate. A smaller moon in the background. Pale warm stellar backlight near the planet's horizon. Show a world taking shape in deep space, not a computer interface or an educational solar-system diagram.
Use the same sophisticated matte game-key-art finish as the references. Deep navy, dark steel blue, cyan atmosphere, a restrained warm highlight. Less saturated and less star-dense than image 1. No bright central yellow sun, no row of many equal-sized solar-system planets. Put the focal planet and wireframe cue within the central 60 percent, keep the upper two-thirds visually strong, and keep the bottom third very dark and calm so game card text remains readable. Must work cropped into both portrait cards and a wide selected-mode card.
Landscape 3:2, approximately 1536x1024. No text, numbers, labels, UI, buttons, frames, logos or watermark. Preserve the original style-reference images unchanged.
```

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished background art for the Create Your Own Map card in a cinematic space strategy game.
Input image: reference for composition, subject and painterly realistic game-art style. Create a new green-themed variation closely related to this image: one majestic rocky world being shaped, with part of the globe represented by an elegant luminous construction wireframe, two or three fine orbital planning arcs and small moons. Keep the large planet in the upper center-right, the small survey ship subordinate, the warm horizon backlight subtle, and the lower third dark and calm for overlaid card text. Work both as a portrait crop and a wide card.
Color palette: deep black-green space, emerald planetary atmosphere, bright jade-green construction lines and orbital arcs, natural dark green terrain with realistic clouds. Replace the cyan/blue lighting identity with a clear green identity. Preserve realistic terrain, atmospheric depth, strong silhouette and sophisticated restrained painterly finish; avoid a flat overall green filter or neon wash.
Landscape 3:2, approximately 1536x1024. No text, typography, labels, UI, borders, frames, logos or watermark.
```
